**Engineer who designs.** I handle a business's whole online presence — website, Google search, decks, video and social — so the owner deals with one person instead of five vendors. Every project starts with a free, working preview.

**Tagline:** See it first, then decide. · *Pehle dekhiye, phir decide kijiye.*

**The thread:** I turn messy, manual or outdated things into clean systems people trust.
Electronics engineering → automating business processes at a Mumbai startup → running the websites of a university & healthcare group → helping local businesses look and work like serious brands.

## Voice

- Direct, warm, plain English. Short sentences.
- Write for a busy doctor reading on WhatsApp, not for other developers.
- Evidence over adjectives. If a claim can't be backed by a real project, number or quote, cut it.
- No buzzwords: synergy, cutting-edge, passionate, solutions, leverage, world-class.
- A light Hindi touch where it's natural — the tagline, a greeting. The rest is English.

## Before launch — content to supply

`npm run build` lists every `[PLACEHOLDER]` still on the site, page by page. The main ones:

- [x] WhatsApp / phone number and LinkedIn
- [x] Email address (later: a @shubhamnagar.in address — one line in `profile.email`)
- [ ] Instagram and GitHub URLs (optional) — uncomment them in `profile.socials`
- [ ] Your photo — `public/me.jpg`, then `profile.photo: '/me.jpg'`
- [ ] Number of websites shipped — `proof.stats`
- [ ] For each project: permission to name the client, live URL, screenshots, the problem, the result
- [ ] Real testimonials (with permission), or delete the placeholder entries to hide the section
- [ ] Notes: write the two drafts in `src/content/notes/`, then remove `draft: true`
- [ ] Check service timelines and budget ranges match what you actually quote
- [ ] Check the skills list in `about.skills` matches the tools you use
